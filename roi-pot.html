Concept Prototype

Did you actually
eat your money's
worth?

ROI-Pot tallies every plate you order at an all-you-can-eat Korean BBQ against what those cuts would cost at retail — so instead of guessing, you get a real number on who won the meal: you or the house.

Interaction design Korean BBQ AYCE economics
1 Pick your tier — lunch, dinner, or weekend pricing.
2 Drag or tap each plate as it comes off the grill.
3 Watch it land — food actually drops and piles up on your plate.
4 Close out the check to see your retail value vs. what you paid.
A portfolio concept prototype · not affiliated with any restaurant chain
